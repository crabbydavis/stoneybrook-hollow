What ages can attend Stoneybrook Community school?

We enroll children ages 3 - 6 in our Preschool & Kindergarten program.. Children must be 3 years old and potty trained by September 1st. Preschoolers and kindergarteners are mixed in the same class!

Research has found many benefits to a mixed-aged classroom! Older children have the opportunity to explore academic skills at their own pace while also having the freedom to play. They get to nurture and lead younger children and share their skillsets with them.

Younger children get to learn social and academic skills from older peers. They get to maintain continuity in their education, as they will have the same teacher for 3 years if they begin our program at age 3.

We love our mixed-age classrooms!

Will my child be graded or tested?

We guide children to skills such as mathematics or literacy when they are developmentally ready and interested. When your child is ready, they will learn to master skills at their own pace. We give little large-group instruction.

Going at one's own pace fosters a love of learning. As such, we do not give grades or tests. We provide a variety of documentation for parents to follow along on their child's learning journey. Children are observed during play to determine mastery of skills.

1st - 3rd Grade

What is Stoneybrook’s homeschool hybrid program?

Our homeschool hybrid program is built around the belief that learning should be rich, meaningful, and deeply connected to the world around us.

By prioritizing best educational practices, our program is designed to engage children in hands-on, project-based experiences that are relevant and exciting. We want every child to build a strong sense of community, where collaboration and meaningful relationships are at the core of their daily interactions.

Through local exploration and real-world connections, students develop their sense of citizenship, which fosters a love for our community and their place within it.

Above all, our program strives to ignite a lifelong love of learning and personal growth, encouraging children to embrace challenges and discover the joy of childhood.

What is the homeschool hybrid schedule?

We have 3 scheduling options:

  • Explorers Club
  • Wayfinders Program
  • Explorers and Wayfinders combined
 What is the Explorers Club?

The Explorers Club is held every Monday from September - May.

Explorers Club is a one-day program designed to cultivate curiosity, independence, and a love of learning. Through forest exploration, free play, outings, and open- ended inquiry in science, social studies, math, and language, children develop the mindset and skills to direct their own learning. Rather than following a structured academic scope and sequence, this program encourages children to explore their interests and grow socially, emotionally, and physically in a supportive, nature-rich environment.

What is the Wayfinders Program?

Wayfinders is a three-day program designed to provide a rich, engaging education that balances structured academics with hands-on exploration. In addition to the freedom, creativity, and discovery of the Explorers Club, Wayfinders includes personalized math and language instruction tailored to each child's skill level. Social Studies, Science, and Makerspace days provide opportunities for deep inquiry and critical thinking. Rooted in best practices and aligned with the Utah Core Standards, Wayfinders ensures that children receive a comprehensive education while fostering curiosity, independence, and a love of learning.

Although we provide a robust education at three days a week, we encourage families to continue math and language instruction the rest of the week. Learn why under “Do students have homework?”

Why do you follow the Utah Core?

We believe that every child has the right to a full and well-rounded education. The Utah Core Standards were carefully developed by researchers, educators, and child development experts to ensure that students receive a thoughtfully sequenced and comprehensive education. These standards provide a strong foundation in essential subjects while allowing flexibility for creative, hands-on, and nature-based learning.

We know that some homeschooling families may feel disenfranchised by traditional public education, but our goal is to show that the Utah Core is not about rigid instruction—it’s about ensuring that children have access to the knowledge and skills they deserve. By following these research-backed guidelines, we can follow each child's progress and provide families with confidence that their children are receiving a rich, meaningful, and high-quality education.

Take a look at the standards for yourself - we hope that you find they are reasonable and helpful in protecting a child’s right to a full education. We will differ from these standards only in regards to the pace at which a child may move. We believe in mastery learning. Only when a child has gained a skill will they move on, as opposed to moving through content at the same pace as other peers their age.

What ages/grades does the program serve?

We serve First - Third graders (ages 6-9) in one mixed-age class.

Mixed-age classrooms create a collaborative learning environment where younger students learn from older peers, and older students reinforce their knowledge through mentorship. This structure fosters independence, social-emotional growth, and a strong sense of community while reducing competition. By mirroring real-world social dynamics, mixed-age learning helps children develop communication, leadership, and problem-solving skills that extend beyond the classroom.

 What teaching philosophy do you follow?

Stoneybrook is Reggio Emilia-inspired with a strong emphasis on nature-based, project-based, and play-driven learning. We encourage curiosity, creativity, and hands-on exploration. We facilitate projects based on emergent curriculum. Teachers observe children during play and discussions to determine their current interests - our curriculum emerges from the children. Projects and investigations help the children dive into their interests further, honing the skills they’re working on during learning pods and at home.

How are students grouped?

We have one multi-age classroom that promotes mentorship, collaboration, and individualized learning experiences. During our learning pods, children will be grouped based on skill level to make sure they are getting a “just right” challenge.

Do students have homework?

We encourage families to read together daily and protect unstructured playtime, both of which are essential for cognitive and social development (Sénéchal & Young, 2008). While we don’t typically send work home, we recommend incorporating additional math and language activities at home to reinforce learning, as consistent exposure to these subjects strengthens long-term retention and confidence.

Research shows that daily reading builds vocabulary, comprehension, and critical thinking skills (National Reading Panel, 2000), while regular math practice enhances problem-solving abilities and mathematical fluency (Boaler, 2015; (Clements & Sarama, 2011). We will share what we’re working on in math and language so you can support your child’s growth at home in a way that feels natural and engaging.
